AI in the Workplace: New course to equip future tech talent

Description Datacom has now reached 42,000+ enrolments for the virtual work experience ‘job simulations’ it provides via the Forage platform – and some of the graduates have joined the team.
  • Datacom’s free virtual job simulations on Forage have reached 42,000+ enrolments and are opening real pathways into tech careers.
  • The programme now includes six courses, including a new AI-focused simulation designed to build in-demand, practical digital skills.
  • Learners of all backgrounds are gaining confidence and capabilities, with strong course ratings and some participants progressing into roles at Datacom.

Datacom now has six virtual work experience simulations available online, with the launch of its latest “Partnering with AI in the workplace” course, designed to build confidence in critical, in-demand tech skills.

Hosted on Forage, Datacom’s free, open-access courses or job simulations are designed to spark interest in technology careers and provide some practical upskilling in critical areas and to date have reached over 42,000 enrolments since launching in 2023.

Datacom initially developed job simulations for four in-demand pathways – cloud, automation, cybersecurity and service desk – and is now expanding its offering with the launch of a new AI job simulation “Partnering with AI in the workplace”, designed to help learners build foundational skills in one of the fastest-growing areas of tech.

“We created these courses to break down barriers and show people that a career in tech is not only possible – it’s exciting, creative and full of opportunity,” says Laura Malcolm, Managing Director of Datacom Australia.

The courses have been developed for high school students, but enrolments show they appeal to a wide audience.
“We’ve seen high school students, university learners, career changers and people with no prior experience all benefit from the platform. It’s helping people build confidence and skills they can take into any role,” says Malcolm.

Datacom is also proving the courses can open doors and has hired a number of people, who have previously completed the virtual job simulations, into roles with the organisation including a CSR, End User Support role, Associate Developer, and 24x7 Data Centre Site Lead.

The new “Partnering with AI in the workplace” job simulation introduces learners to the practical skill of prompt engineering – the ability to guide AI tools effectively to solve problems, communicate clearly and work smarter. Learners also explore de-bugging techniques, helping them understand how to troubleshoot and refine AI outputs.

“AI is no longer a niche skill – it is being used as a tool across every industry,” Lou Compagnone, Director of AI at Datacom. “We designed this simulation to help people understand how to collaborate with AI, not just use it. It’s about learning how to ask the right questions, think critically and apply judgement – skills that employers are actively looking for.”

Malcolm says the “Partnering with AI in the workplace” job simulation is extremely timely given the insights shared in Datacom’s report The Productivity Pivot; AI, People and the Future of Work, which illustrates how critical AI skills are in boosting productivity and the fact that over 70% of people are want to improve their tech skills and believe AI will boost their efficiency.

Datacom’s virtual work experience platform is part of a broader commitment to developing local tech talent across Australia and New Zealand. The programme is delivered via Forage, a global virtual experience platform that has hosted more than 5 million student enrolments.

Among those learners who have completed Datacom’s job simulations, the average rating for these short online courses is 4.5 out of 5.

Participants who excel in the simulations may be invited to apply for Datacom’s early careers opportunities, creating a clear pathway from learning to employment.

“As Australia and New Zealand’s largest homegrown tech company, we’re proud to help grow the digital skills our communities need,” says Malcolm. “This platform is a stepping stone for anyone curious about tech – and we’re just getting started.”

Datacom’s job simulations on Forage:

  • Newly launched module
    Partnering with AI in the Workplace: Explore how AI can enhance productivity and problem-solving in a cross-functional team at Datacom. Learn prompt writing, AI-assisted research and how to communicate insights effectively.
  • Automation AI Accelerator: From Co-pilot to Autonomous Agent – learn what it's like to work as an automation developer at Datacom, including effective problem solving, AI automation and client management.
  • Introduction to Cloud: Learn what it's like to work in Datacom's cloud solutions team. From basic cloud concepts to learning how to think about real-world problems cloud can solve.
  • Cybersecurity: Learn how to navigate scenarios cybersecurity professionals handle on a daily basis – from risk assessment, threat research to data analysis, and how to craft actionable recommendations.
  • Service Desk: Learn how to resolve technical issues and when to escalate in Datacom's service desk job simulation.
